# Non-UPF Verified trademark usage

**Program:** Non-UPF Verified
**Brand context:** `data-brand="nonupf"`
**Source:** *Non-UPF Verified Trademark Use Guide* v1.0, published December 2025. Where this page and the guide differ, the guide wins.
**For:** Licensees with a signed license agreement and Non-UPF Verified products.
**Contact:** marketing@nonultraprocessed.org
**Page:** `trademark/non-upf-verified.html`

How to use the Non-UPF Verified mark on packaging, websites and marketing. The mark tells shoppers that a product has met every requirement of the Non-UPF Verified Standard. Using it the same way everywhere builds the trust that makes it useful.

## Before you start

- **Only verified products carry the mark.** The program verifies individual products, not brands, companies or product families. Every product shown or named near the mark must be Non-UPF Verified.
- **You need a signed license agreement** that grants permission to use the trademarks. Unauthorized use is trademark infringement and a breach of the license agreement.
- **Never use the mark before verification and approval.** It is strictly prohibited.
- **Keep it separate from other programs.** The Non-UPF Verified trademarks may not be swapped for, combined with or represented by logos, icons or phrases from related programs, including the Non-GMO Project and the Food Integrity Collective. Each program has its own trademarks and guide.
- **Other uses need approval.** Any use beyond verified products, such as media, education or promotion, needs approval first. Questions about packaging, placement or other uses go to marketing@nonultraprocessed.org.

## Required parts

Every mark has four parts. All four are required, and none may be removed or rearranged:

1. The icon
2. The NON UPF VERIFIED wordmark
3. The URL: nonultraprocessed.org
4. The keyline

## Clear space and size

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Clear space | Keep a space equal to the height of the capital "N" in "Non-UPF" on every side, measured from the outside of the keyline. No text or graphic may touch the mark or enter that space. The space scales with the mark |
| Minimum size, primary mark, print | 3/8 inch tall and 1/2 inch wide |
| Minimum size, secondary mark, print | 0.375 inch wide |
| Minimum size, primary mark, digital | 80 pixels wide |
| Minimum size, secondary mark, digital | 60 pixels wide |
| URL | Must be readable at every allowed size |

## Placement and brand hierarchy

- The mark must never be larger or more prominent than your brand's primary logo. It supports your brand identity and should not compete with it.
- Place it cleanly where it can be read. Never put it in a busy or visually complex area.
- Never place it where it could look like part of the product name or your brand identity.

## Color

The mark always appears in **one solid color**. No gradients, shading, patterns or multi-color treatments.

The official files come in the program colors, black and white. These are preferred:

| Color | Pantone | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Pink | 214 C | `#CE0F69` |
| Navy | 282 C | `#041E42` |
| White | | `#FFFFFF` |

Brands with verified products may also reproduce the mark in **any single solid color** that suits their packaging or gives better contrast. Every part of the mark must be in that one color; you may not color parts separately.

## Transparency and backgrounds

- The mark keeps a fully transparent background so the package shows through it.
- It can sit on a patterned background only if it stays clearly legible. Avoid low-contrast areas, gradients, photographs and busy regions.
- If a pattern or graphic makes it hard to read, you may place the mark on a solid color block that fits your package design. The block may only be used to keep the mark readable and must not change or hide any part of it.

## Not permitted

- Multi-color versions
- Changing the mark's proportions
- Editing or removing the URL
- Redrawing the icon or using new artwork in its place
- Adding outlines, drop shadows, bevels or filters
- Placing the mark inside another shape or badge
- Using the icon alone without approval
- Rotating, skewing, stretching or warping
- Animating the mark
- Using the mark as a watermark or a repeating background pattern
- Cropping any part of the mark

## Websites

The mark applies to verified products, not companies or organizations.

- Brands that carry the mark on verified products may also show it next to those products' online listings.
- Consider a webpage with its own URL about your verified products and why non-ultraprocessed options matter to you. The Non-UPF Verified team can help with content and review on request.

## Marketing materials

The trademarks must never be confused with the business name or a product's brand name, or suggest that the program endorses anything it doesn't cover. When the mark shares a layout with your branding:

- It never appears more prominently than your primary branding.
- It never implies company-wide verification or endorsement of something the program doesn't cover.
- It sits smaller than, and below, your corporate logos.

## Documents and brand communications

The mark always refers to verified products, not whole brands, companies or organizations.

- **Documents** such as a company report, product sheet, summary or value statement may use the mark on the pages about Non-UPF Verified products only.
- **Brand-wide communications** may use the mark more widely if Non-UPF verification covers your entire product line, with a clear statement that it does.

Examples of brand-wide communications:

- Branded email signatures
- Social media profiles
- Digital presentation decks
- Business cards
- Event displays

## Press

Press materials must use only the approved trademarks, avoid confusion with other certifications or campaigns, and refer only to verified products. For help with media language or a review, contact marketing@nonultraprocessed.org.

## Accurate and truthful claims

Labels on verified products must be accurate, truthful and not misleading.

| Claim | Rule |
|---|---|
| In process | Never promote "in process" status on packaging or marketing. You may talk about working toward verification in conversation, but never next to the mark |
| UPF free | "Non-UPF Verified" tells shoppers the product complies with the Non-UPF Verified Standard. Any other claim, such as "UPF free", "zero UPFs" or "100% no UPFs", must be accurate, truthful, not misleading and lawful. You are responsible for that |
| First and only | Not allowed, for example "first Non-UPF Verified potato chip" or "only Non-UPF Verified pasta". With many technical administrators verifying thousands of products, these claims cannot be confirmed |
| Competing certifications | No other non-UPF certification may appear with the Non-UPF Verified trademark. Plain text on packaging that is not tied to another organization is fine |
| Bioengineered food disclosure | Never use the trademarks on any product, display or material that also carries a bioengineered food disclosure under the National Bioengineered Food Disclosure Standard (NBFDS) |

## Questions

The Non-UPF Verified team monitors use of the trademarks and acts to protect their integrity. For help with messaging, packaging or marketing materials, contact marketing@nonultraprocessed.org.
